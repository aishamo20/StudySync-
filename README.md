# 📚 StudySync: Smart Study Environment Assistant

*A low-cost Arduino desk device that keeps your study space comfortable and reminds you to take care of yourself.*

## The problem
Students study for long stretches and often don't notice when their room
is too hot, cold or dry, or forget to take breaks and drink water.

## What it does
- Monitors temperature and humidity in real time
- Shows the status on an LCD display, with a green LED when conditions are
  ideal and a yellow LED when they're not
- Sends timed hydration and break reminders with a buzzer
- Lets users adjust timings with push buttons
- Works fully offline, with no phone needed (so fewer distractions)

## Built with
Arduino UNO · DHT11 sensor · 16x2 LCD · LEDs · buzzer · push buttons ·
Arduino IDE · C/C++ · Tinkercad (simulation)

Estimated prototype cost: £61–£110

## Supporting the UN SDGs
- **SDG 4, Quality Education:** better study environments support focus
- **SDG 3, Good Health and Wellbeing:** promotes hydration and regular breaks
